Partnership models

How we can work together

We keep this flexible on purpose — the right model depends on your business, not the other way around.

No required fixed costs

Joining VIBIN's catalogue doesn't require a mandatory monthly fee. Commercial terms — if any — are agreed per partner, based on the model below that fits you.

01

Affiliate / commission

You pay only on results — a commission on bookings that actually came from VIBIN, tracked end-to-end. Rate agreed directly with you; we don't apply one blanket percentage to every partner.

02

Referral / tracking

We send you tracked, attributed traffic and report on it — clicks, and conversions where your systems can confirm them. A lighter-weight starting point before any commercial terms are discussed.

03

Custom partnership

Featured placement, seasonal promotion, or something specific to your business — we shape the details together rather than fitting you into a fixed package.

04 — OPEN

Propose your own model

Already have a partnership structure that works for you elsewhere? Bring it. We'd rather adapt to a model that fits your business than force one that doesn't.

Getting started

What we need from you to get your activity live and accurate.

  1. Tell us a bit about the business — name, activity type, and your official website.
  2. Share accurate activity details: description, pricing, opening hours, and the right booking or ticket link.
  3. Send a few real photos of the actual experience — interior, activity in progress, not just the storefront.
  4. Name a contact person for questions and future updates.
  5. Tell us which partnership model (above) you'd like to explore — or propose your own.
